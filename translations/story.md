Horn of the Warlord — Campaign Design Document
1. Core Concept

Horn of the Warlord is a turn-based strategy campaign set in a Viking-era world where survival evolves into conquest.

The player leads a Viking faction that crosses the sea in search of wealth—but quickly shifts from raiding to claiming land. This triggers war with established kingdoms and conflict within Viking culture itself.

Core Theme
Survival → Expansion → Rule
Raider → Conqueror → Warlord
Central Question

Will you remain a Viking… or become something greater—and risk losing what made you one?

6. Missions
Mission 1 — Building the Storm
Description

On Ravenmark Isle, harsh homeland of Clan Ravenmark, Jarl Eirik Stormborn has sounded the Horn of the Warlord. The clan gathers to prepare for a voyage beyond the known sea. Before ships can sail, the settlement must grow, and warriors must be raised for what lies ahead.

Intro

On Ravenmark Isle, the homeland of Clan Ravenmark, longhouses cling to cold stone and wind-beaten shores. This land has never given freely. It feeds the stubborn and tests the rest.

For many winters, the clan has endured — fighting over thin soil, empty waters, and fading fortune. But now the old burial mound has been opened, and from it came a relic long forgotten: the Horn of the Warlord.

Jarl Eirik Stormborn raised it before the gathered clan and sounded it across the island. Its call carried over cliffs and forests, stirring something long buried in the hearts of his people.

Not a raid for survival. Not a voyage for trade.

Something greater.

Beyond the known sea lies land untouched by Ravenmark blades. The clan will not wait for hunger to take them. They will cross the waters and take what they need.

But first, they must be ready.

Outro

Ravenmark Isle no longer stands quiet. Fires burn through the night, and the sound of hammer and axe echoes across the shore. What was once a struggling settlement now prepares for war.

Warriors gather, their shields painted, their weapons forged anew. The longships are loaded with all the clan can spare — and all it dares to risk.

Jarl Eirik Stormborn stands at the water’s edge as the Horn of the Warlord sounds once more. Not as a call to gather… but as a call to depart.

The ships push into open water.

Behind them lies the land that could no longer sustain them.

Ahead lies something unknown.

The first step has been taken.

Mission 2 — Blood on New Shores
Description

The ships of Clan Ravenmark reach Veyr, a rugged island claimed by the Kingdom of Caerwold. Though small, it is inhabited and protected, a first barrier before the lands beyond. Jarl Eirik Stormborn must lead the landing, strike fast, and break the island’s resistance before it can organize.

Intro

After days at sea, the waters finally give way to land.

Veyr emerges through mist and spray — a hard island of cliffs, grass, scattered dwellings, and worn paths. It belongs to the Kingdom of Caerwold, a realm said to be rich, ordered, and strong.

But this is not that realm. This is its edge.

The longships drift along the shore as warriors prepare to land. No army stands ready. Only a quiet land that does not yet understand what has come.

Jarl Eirik Stormborn looks across the island.

He raises his hand.

The Horn of the Warlord sounds.

Event 1

The sea carries the Ravenmark fleet along the edge of Veyr. Warriors grip their shields in silence, waiting for the moment their boots touch foreign ground.

Right-click on a region to land your army.

Event 2

Torvald Ironhand brings a second warband alongside Eirik’s fleet, ready to strike a different part of Veyr and tear the island apart from within.

Right-click on a region to land your second army.

Outro

By the time the fighting ends, Veyr is no longer what it was. Smoke drifts across the land, and the last resistance has been broken.

The mark of Caerwold has been driven out. In its place stands the presence of Ravenmark.

The warriors gather what they came for — food, tools, wealth — the spoils of a successful raid. But Jarl Eirik Stormborn does not turn back to the ships.

He looks across the island, then beyond it.

This land was taken quickly. Too quickly to abandon.

What began as a raid now becomes something more.

A foothold.
